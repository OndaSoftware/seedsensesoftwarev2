If you are the first person from your company in SeedSense, a little setup up
front saves a lot of correcting later. Everything here lives under **Settings →
Data settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Crops and classifications">
    Add the crops you work with. Traits and evaluations hang off these, so they
    come first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Regions, states, and areas">
    These are how trials get located and how work is divided between people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farmers and suppliers">
    Add the growers whose ground you trial on, and the suppliers whose varieties
    you evaluate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teams and members">
    Invite your evaluators and put them on teams so tasks can be assigned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="tip">
  You can add reference data at any time. Start with the crops and regions you
  will use this season and leave the rest.
</Callout>
