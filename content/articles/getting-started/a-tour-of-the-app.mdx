SeedSense is the same product on every device, but it is not the same shape. The
browser gives you the whole application; the mobile app gives you the part that
happens standing in a field.

## On the web

A sidebar down the left, seven entries:

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Your seed trial tasks |
| Trial Input | Entering and submitting a new trial |
| Trial Catalog | Browsing and managing your trials |
| Evaluation Catalog | Reviewing variety evaluations across trials |
| Variety Catalog | Exploring and comparing varieties |
| Data Settings | Crops, zones, teams, and other reference data |
| Settings | Your account and preferences |

## On mobile

Three tabs along the bottom:

**Tasks List** is home — Today, Next 3 Days, Past Due, and All. **Trials Map**
plots your trials so you can see what is nearby. **Data Settings** is the same
reference data as the web.

There is no separate catalog on mobile. You reach a trial by tapping one of its
tasks, and everything about that trial — details, varieties, layout, tasks,
reports — is on the screen that opens.

## Which device for which job

Setting up trials, importing varieties, working the catalogs, and running
reports are all far easier in a browser. Scoring varieties, taking photos, and
working through today's list are what the phone and tablet are for.

See [Get started on the mobile app](/getting-started/the-mobile-app).

## Reading sync state

Trials are marked as synced or unsynced. Unsynced means the change is safely on
your device but has not reached the server yet — see
[How syncing works](/data-and-sync/how-syncing-works).
