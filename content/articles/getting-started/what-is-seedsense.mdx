SeedSense keeps seed trial work in one place, from planning a trial through to
grading the varieties in it. Almost everything you do sits inside one of five
ideas, and once those click the rest of the app is navigation.

## The five ideas

**Trials** are the unit of work. A trial covers one planting at one location,
and holds everything else on this list.

**Varieties** are what you are comparing. You assign them to a trial, and they
appear in its layout and in every evaluation.

**Tasks** are the field work a trial needs — planting, germination checks,
thinning, maturity checks, field meetings — each with a date and an evaluator.

**Evaluations** are the scores. Evaluators rate varieties against traits, and
those scores roll up into a grade.

**Sync** is what moves your field work to the server. You can work with no
signal at all; the app holds your changes and sends them when you reconnect.

<Callout variant="tip">
  You do not have to set everything up before your first planting. Create a
  trial, add the varieties you know about, and fill in the rest as you go.
</Callout>
