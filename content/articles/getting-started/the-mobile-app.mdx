The mobile app is built for the part of the job that happens in the field:
working through today's tasks, scoring varieties in front of the plot, and
taking photos. Setting up trials and running reports is easier in a browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    See [Install on your phone, tablet, or browser](/getting-started/install-on-phone-tablet-or-web)
    for where to get it on each device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use the email address your administrator invited. If you have never set a
    password, open the invitation email and use **Set Up Your Password** first —
    that link expires after seven days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your task list">
    The app opens on **Tasks List**, showing the field work for the trials
    assigned to you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The three tabs

**Tasks List** is home. It has four tabs of its own — Today, Next 3 Days, Past
Due, and All — and every task on it belongs to a trial you are the evaluator
for. Tapping a task opens that trial.

**Trials Map** plots your trials on a map, which is the quickest way to work
out what is near you and how to get there.

**Data Settings** is the same reference data you see on the web — crops,
varieties, farmers, zones, suppliers, traits, and teams. See
[Managing data settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings).

## Working a trial from a task

Opening a task takes you to the trial, where everything about it sits on one
screen: the trial's own details, its varieties, its layout, its tasks, and the
reports section.

To score a variety, open **Trial Varieties**, pick the variety, and work through
the evaluation. See [Score a variety](/evaluations/score-a-variety).

<Callout variant="tip">
  A tablet is the sweet spot for field work — the forms have room to breathe,
  and it still fits in the truck.
</Callout>

## What needs a signal

Recording work does not. Reports and emails do — generating a trial map,
generating an evaluations report, and sending emails all run on the server, and
the app tells you when there is no connection rather than failing quietly.

Anything you record offline is stored on the device and uploaded on the next
sync. See [Working offline](/data-and-sync/working-offline).
