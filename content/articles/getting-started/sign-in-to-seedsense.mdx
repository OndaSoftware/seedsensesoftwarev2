SeedSense accounts are created by your organization, not by signing up. Your
administrator adds your email address, and you receive an invitation to set a
password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    It comes from the SeedSense address and contains a link that is good for a
    limited time. If it has expired, ask your administrator to resend it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Choose something you can type on a phone with gloves on — you will be
    entering it in the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter the same email address the invitation was sent to. Sign-in is tied to
    that address, not to your name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you forget your password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen. The reset link goes to the same
address, so check spam if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

If you are stuck on the sign-in screen entirely, see
[I can't sign in](/troubleshooting/cant-sign-in).
