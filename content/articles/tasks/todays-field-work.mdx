Home opens on your task load. Four tabs: **All**, **Past Due**, **Today**, and
**Next Three Days**.

Only tasks on trials where you are the evaluator appear here, and completed
tasks drop off — so what is left is genuinely outstanding.

## Planning a day

Next Three Days is the useful one for planning. Trials cluster geographically, so
pulling tomorrow's checks forward into today's trip is often worth more than
being exactly on schedule.

The mobile **trials map** is the other half of this. Tasks sit on the trial they
belong to, so three checks due the same day across a wide area read as a route:
hit this one first, drive over and hit that one, then the last. Deciding the
order on a map beats working down a list.

## Task colors

Tasks are colored by type — planting, germination, thinning, overall check,
maturity check, field meeting, and other — and the same colors carry through
wherever tasks appear.

<Callout variant="note">
  Every color is paired with a label everywhere it appears. You never have to
  distinguish tasks by color alone.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip">
  An empty list usually means the trials you expect are assigned to somebody
  else, not that there is no work — see
  [Assign tasks to evaluators](/tasks/assign-tasks-to-evaluators).
</Callout>
