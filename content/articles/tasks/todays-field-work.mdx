Home opens on your task load, split three ways: due today, due in the next three
days, and past due.

## Planning a day

The next-three-days list is the useful one for planning. Trials cluster
geographically, so pulling tomorrow's checks forward into today's trip is often
worth more than being exactly on schedule.

## Task colors

Tasks are colored by type — planting, germination, thinning, overall check,
maturity check, field meeting — and the same colors appear on the trial map, so
a plot's outstanding work is visible without opening it.

<Callout variant="note">
  Every color is paired with a label everywhere it appears. You never have to
  distinguish tasks by color alone.
</Callout>
