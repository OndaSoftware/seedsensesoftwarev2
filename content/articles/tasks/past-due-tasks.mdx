A task goes past due when its due date passes without being completed. **Past
Due** is a tab of its own on the home screen, which is deliberate — it is hard to
ignore.

## Clearing it

Three things clear a past due task:

- **Complete it.** Doing the work and ticking it off moves it to Completed and
  takes it off the home screen. The record of what was done and when survives.
- **Reschedule it.** Change the date to one that reflects reality.
- **Delete it.** Right for a task created by mistake, or one that has genuinely
  been overtaken — but deleting leaves no trace it ever existed, so prefer
  completing where the work actually happened.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Resist rescheduling a germination or maturity check to a much later date just
  to clear the list. The window matters, and a check taken well outside it is
  worse than a recorded gap.
</Callout>
