A task goes past due when its date passes without being completed. The list is
deliberately hard to ignore.

## Clearing it

Three things clear a past due task: doing the work, rescheduling it to a date
that reflects reality, or marking it as not needed.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Resist rescheduling a germination or maturity check to a much later date just
  to clear the list. The window matters, and a check taken well outside it is
  worse than a recorded gap.
</Callout>
