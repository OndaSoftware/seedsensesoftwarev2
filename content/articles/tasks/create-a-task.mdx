Tasks belong to a trial. Open a trial, scroll to **Trial Tasks**, and choose
**Manage Tasks** — everything below happens there.

Tasks are reminders, not requirements. A trial works perfectly well without a
single one. They exist so that by the time a season is over, everything you
meant to do actually got done.

The task list has three tabs: **Active**, **Completed**, and **All**.

## Adding a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a task type">
    Planting, Germination, Thinning, Overall Check, Maturity Check, Evaluation,
    Field Meeting, or Other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the due date">
    One date, not a range. Pick the start of the week you expect to be there
    rather than the exact day — a germination check three weeks out is a window
    in practice, and the date is what puts it in front of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description">
    Optional, and the place to say what "Overall Check" means on this trial:
    *check the transplants made it*, *check for germ*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The task appears immediately on the trial, and on the evaluator's task list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add as many as you like to one trial. Two working patterns both hold up:
set the whole season out at once and tick items off, or keep one live task per
trial and roll it forward — overall check becomes maturity check becomes
harvest.

## Where tasks show up

A task follows the trial **and** sticks to the evaluator, so it lands in two
places:

- On that evaluator's **home screen** as the due date approaches.
- On the **mobile trials map**, positioned on the trial itself. Three tasks due
  the same day across a wide area become a driving route you can plan instead of
  a list you work through in whatever order it happens to be in.

## Editing and completing

Opening an existing task gives you the same fields plus a **Mark as Complete**
toggle. Completing a task moves it from the Active tab to Completed and takes it
off the home screen; it is not deleted, so the record of what was done and when
survives.

Tasks can also be deleted outright. That is fine for one created by mistake, and
some people delete rather than complete once the work is done — but completing
keeps the history, and deleting does not. See
[Past due tasks](/tasks/past-due-tasks).

## Who sees the task

Whoever is set as the trial's **evaluator**. There is no separate assignment
step on the task itself — see
[Assign tasks to evaluators](/tasks/assign-tasks-to-evaluators).

<Callout variant="note">
  Tasks are internal. They are never shown to the grower or to suppliers, and
  they do not appear on the trial map or evaluations report. Write them for
  yourself.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip">
  Set the whole season's tasks when you create the trial. It takes a few minutes
  once, and it means the task list is a real plan rather than a reminder written
  the day before.
</Callout>
