Field work happens where there is no signal. SeedSense is built for that: open
the task, record what you see, and the app holds it until you reconnect.

## Working through a trial

Opening a task from the trial map walks you through plots in layout order.
Scores save as you go — you do not have to finish the whole trial in one pass.

## Photos

Photos attach to the evaluation and upload with it. They are the largest thing
the app syncs, so a trial with many photos takes noticeably longer to finish
uploading.

## Marking it complete

The **Mark as Complete** toggle is on the task itself. Completing it moves it out
of Active and off your home screen, without deleting the record — see
[Manage tasks for a trial](/tasks/create-a-task).

<Callout variant="warning">
  The catalogs do not refresh on their own, even with signal. Hit **Sync** when
  you get back into service, or work you did in the field sits on the device
  looking like it never happened.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip">
  Before leaving a site, check the trial catalog's unsynced filter. It takes
  five seconds and tells you whether anything is still waiting.
</Callout>
