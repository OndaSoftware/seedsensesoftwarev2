There is no "assign to" field on a task, and looking for one is the usual first
wrong turn.

**A task belongs to a trial, and a trial has one evaluator.** Every task on that
trial appears on that evaluator's task list, and on nobody else's.

## Reassigning work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trial, not the task">
    From the trial catalog on the web, or by tapping any of its tasks on mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the evaluator">
    The dropdown lists the people in your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Every task on the trial moves with it — past due, active, and completed
    alike.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="warning">
  Reassigning is all-or-nothing. You cannot leave last month's completed checks
  with one person and hand the rest to another; the trial and its whole task
  history move together.
</Callout>

## Splitting work between people

Because assignment is at the trial level, two people working the same trial
means one of them is working from someone else's task list. That is usually
fine — the work still records against the trial under whoever is signed in.

If you need the split to be visible, the cleaner option is a
[team](/account-and-settings/teams-and-team-members), which also gives you a
recipient group when emailing that trial's reports.

## When someone's list looks empty

Their trials are assigned to somebody else. Check the evaluator on a trial you
expect them to see — see
[A trial or variety is missing](/troubleshooting/missing-trials-or-varieties).
