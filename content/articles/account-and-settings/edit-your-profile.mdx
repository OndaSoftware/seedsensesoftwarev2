Your profile lives under **Settings**. Your name is what colleagues see against
tasks you are assigned and evaluations you record, so it is worth having it read
the way people refer to you.

## Changing your password

Change it from the profile screen while signed in. If you cannot sign in at all,
use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen instead.

<Callout variant="note">
  Your email address is your sign-in identity and is managed by your
  administrator. Ask them if it needs to change.
</Callout>
