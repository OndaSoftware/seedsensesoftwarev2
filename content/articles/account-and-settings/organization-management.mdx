Organization settings cover the things shared by everyone: the reference data,
the team list, and the defaults new trials inherit.

## Who can change what

Administrators manage the organization and its members. Evaluators can record
work and read the data they have access to, but cannot change organization-level
settings.

If you need a setting changed and cannot see it, you are almost certainly not an
administrator — ask whoever set up your organization.
