SeedSense sends two kinds of email: account mail such as invitations and
password resets, and trial mail such as reports to farmers and suppliers.

## Team emails

Team email addresses control who on your side is copied on trial
correspondence.

## Farmer emails

Farmer addresses are attached to the farmer record, not the trial, so updating
one address fixes every trial on that grower's ground.

<Callout variant="tip">
  If someone reports missing SeedSense email, have them check spam first — it is
  the cause far more often than a wrong address.
</Callout>
