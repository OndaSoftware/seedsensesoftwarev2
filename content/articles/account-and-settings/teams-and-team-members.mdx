Teams are how people are grouped for assignment and reporting. Someone must be
on your organization before tasks can be assigned to them.

## Inviting someone

Add their email address and they receive an invitation to set a password. Until
they accept, they exist but cannot sign in.

## When someone leaves

Remove them from the team rather than deleting anything. Their past evaluations
stay attached to their name, which is what you want for an audit trail.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Reassign a departing evaluator's outstanding tasks before removing them, or
  that work drops off everyone's home screen.
</Callout>
