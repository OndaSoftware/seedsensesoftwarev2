An evaluation is recorded against one variety in one trial. Open the trial, go
to **Trial Varieties**, and pick the variety you are standing in front of.

## The form, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Maturity date">
    The date this variety actually reached maturity, which is what the days-to-
    maturity figures across the app are built from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Traits">
    Choose **Manage Evaluation Traits** to score the traits attached to this
    crop. Each trait accepts the answers set up for it in Data Settings — a
    rating, or one of a fixed list of options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Score">
    An overall 1 to 10 for the variety. This is the number that drives the
    averages in the variety catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluation grade">
    Your verdict — see [Grades explained](/evaluations/grades-explained).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pros, cons, and conclusion">
    Free text. This is what a reader of the report will actually read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Photos">
    Take them from the camera or add from the gallery. They appear in the
    evaluations report and on the variety's page in the catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as Complete">
    Until you do, the evaluation counts as in progress and is excluded from the
    completed-evaluation figures.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the trait list is empty

The traits shown come from the trial's crop. An empty list means no traits are
attached to that crop yet — see [Crop traits](/evaluations/crop-traits).

## AI analysis

Where it is available, the app can draft the pros, cons, and conclusion from the
traits and scores you have already entered. It fills the fields in for you to
edit; nothing is submitted on your behalf. Let it finish before switching to
another evaluation.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Score the check variety in the same visit. Every comparison downstream — the
  report, the catalog charts — reads against it, and a check scored a week later
  in different weather is worth less than one scored beside its neighbours.
</Callout>

## Offline

Evaluations record with no signal, photos included, and upload on the next sync.
See [Working offline](/data-and-sync/working-offline).
