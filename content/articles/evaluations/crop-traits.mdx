Traits are attached to crops. When an evaluator opens a trial, the app shows the
traits configured for that trial's crop and nothing else.

## Setting them up

Under **Data settings**, open the crop and choose its traits. Adding a trait
mid-season is fine — it appears in evaluations from that point on, and earlier
evaluations simply have no value for it.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Removing a trait from a crop hides it from future evaluations but does not
  delete scores already recorded against it.
</Callout>
