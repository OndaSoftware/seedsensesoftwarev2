A trait is one thing you measure — vigor, uniformity, disease resistance, head
size. Its attributes define how it is scored.

## Attributes

Attributes control the scale and the allowed values. A trait scored one to nine
and a trait scored present or absent behave differently in the field and in the
results, and that difference is set here.

## Keep the list short

The single biggest factor in how long an evaluation takes is how many traits it
asks for. Trim the list to what you will actually act on — evaluators score more
consistently when there is less to score.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Order traits the way an evaluator naturally looks at a plot: whole plant
  first, then detail. It measurably speeds up scoring.
</Callout>
