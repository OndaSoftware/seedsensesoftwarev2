Where the [trial catalog](/trials/the-trial-catalog) lists trials, the
**Evaluation Catalog** lists evaluations — one row per variety scored, across
every trial. It is the view for questions that span trials rather than sit
inside one.

## Filters

Wider than the trial catalog's, because the questions are wider: crop, variety,
classification, supplier, evaluator, farmer, region, zone, ranch, field,
evaluation grade, planting date range, and maturation date range.

They combine. Crop plus supplier plus grade answers "how did this supplier's
material do for us this season" in three clicks.

## Reading a result

Each evaluation shows its variety, farmer, planting date, and maturity date, and
opens into the full evaluation — traits, score, grade, pros, cons, conclusion,
and photos.

## Exporting

**Export Catalog** offers CSV and PDF, and exports what your filters have
selected. The confirmation dialog lists every filter and the count before
anything is generated.

Two limits worth knowing:

- With no filters applied, there is nothing selected to export — the app asks
  you to filter first rather than exporting everything.
- The PDF export is capped at **100 evaluations** at a time. Past that you are
  asked to narrow the filters, because the report stops being readable.

CSV is the better choice when the answer is going into a spreadsheet anyway.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Filter to one crop and one season before exporting a PDF. It keeps you under
  the cap and produces a document somebody will actually read.
</Callout>
