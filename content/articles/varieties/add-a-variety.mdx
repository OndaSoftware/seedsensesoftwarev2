Varieties live in a shared catalog under **Data Settings → Varieties**, so one
added once can be used by every trial in your organization.

**Add Variety** asks for four things:

**Variety Name** is what evaluators see in the field, so use the name they will
recognise on the seed packet. It has to be unique in your organization.

**Variety Type** is one of Commercial, Dropped, Pre-commercial, Screening,
Slotting, Research, Experimental, or Advanced Research.

**Classification** is the crop classification it belongs to. This is also how
the variety gets its crop — there is no separate crop field.

**Supplier** is who it came from, and it decides which supplier reps are offered
as recipients when you email a trial's reports.

<Callout variant="note">
  If the Classification or Supplier dropdown is empty, that list has to be
  filled in first — see [Managing data settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings).
</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip">
  Adding more than a handful? [Import varieties in bulk](/varieties/bulk-add-varieties)
  is much faster and keeps the spelling consistent.
</Callout>
