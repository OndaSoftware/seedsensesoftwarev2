Varieties live in a shared catalog under **Data Settings → Varieties**, so one
added once can be used by every trial in your organization.

**Add Variety** asks for four things:

**Variety Name** is what evaluators see in the field, so use the name they will
recognise on the seed packet. It has to be unique in your organization.

**Variety Type** is one of Commercial, Dropped, Pre-commercial, Screening,
Slotting, Research, Experimental, or Advanced Research.

**Classification** is the crop classification it belongs to — *Bell Pepper Red*,
*Jalapeño*, *Onion Short Day*. This is also how the variety gets its crop; there
is no separate crop field.

**Supplier** is who it came from, and it decides which supplier reps are offered
as recipients when you email a trial's reports.

## Search before you add

Type the name into the search bar first. A variety entered twice under two
spellings splits its own history in the
[variety catalog](/varieties/the-variety-catalog), and untangling that after
evaluations are attached to both is real work. Duplicate names are rejected on
save, but near-duplicates — a name where somebody else used the breeder number —
are not caught for you.

## Name it so it stays findable

Material arrives as a breeder number and later gets a commercial name. Keep both
in the name field:

```
Ruby Red (BP123)
Hunter - JP1234
```

Parentheses or a dash, either is fine. The point is that the whole field is
searchable, so somebody who only ever knew the variety as `BP123` still finds it
after it is named, and somebody who only knows the commercial name still finds
it too.

This matters more than it sounds. Breeder numbers are how suppliers talk about
material and how your own trial records from three seasons ago refer to it. Drop
the number when the name arrives and that history stops being searchable.

<Callout variant="note">
  If the Classification or Supplier dropdown is empty, that list has to be
  filled in first — see [Managing data settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings).
</Callout>

<Callout variant="tip">
  Adding more than a handful? [Import varieties in bulk](/varieties/bulk-add-varieties)
  is much faster and keeps the spelling consistent.
</Callout>

## Adding one mid-trial

If you are already on a trial and the variety is not in the system, you do not
have to come here — **Add Data Variety** on the trial varieties screen creates it
in place. See
[Assign varieties to a trial](/trials/assign-varieties-to-a-trial).
