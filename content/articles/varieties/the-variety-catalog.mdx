**Variety Catalog** in the sidebar is the long view: not what a variety is, but
how it has performed everywhere you have ever planted it. It aggregates every
evaluation by variety so that a season's field work turns into something you can
make a decision from.

Each variety shows as a card with its crop, classification, and type, plus the
numbers that matter — total trials, completed evaluations, average score,
average days to maturity, and the span of years it has been trialled.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Only **completed** evaluations feed this page. Trait results, overall
  performance, and days to maturity all come from evaluations that have been
  marked complete — an evaluation left in progress contributes nothing here. If
  a variety looks emptier than it should, that is the first thing to check.
</Callout>

## Narrowing the list

Filter by crop, classification, and variety type, and set a minimum number of
trials or completed evaluations to hide anything you have barely tested. Search
by name when you already know what you are looking for, and sort to bring the
best or the longest-running to the top.

## Comparing two varieties

Tick the checkbox in the corner of two cards and choose **Compare Selected**.
Two is the maximum — the comparison is a genuine head-to-head, not a
leaderboard.

The comparison runs the whole page side by side: overall score, days to
maturity, images, evaluator remarks, trait by trait, and trial history. Where
both varieties came out of the same trials it is a true apples-to-apples read,
and the filters apply to both halves at once, so you can narrow to one grower or
one region and watch the answer change.

## Inside a variety

Opening a variety gives you its whole record, with a filter bar across the top —
region, area, zone, grower, planting date range, harvest date range. Every
section below responds to it, so you can ask "how did this do in one region, for
one grower, at one planting time" and have the whole page answer.

**Summary** — trials, completed evaluations, average score, average days to
maturity, how many states it has been trialled in, and the year range.

**Performance vs Check** — overall score and days to maturity by trial location,
plotted against the check variety.

**Trait Results** — the distribution of answers for a chosen trait, switchable
between chart types, with the number of evaluations behind it.

**Field Evaluation Photos** — every photo taken while evaluating it, captioned
with planting and harvest dates, zone, state, and farmer. Click one to open it
full size.

**Trial History** — every trial grouped by year, with each evaluation's score,
pros, cons, and conclusion.

**Trial Locations** — the same trials on a map.

## Evaluator remarks

Every variety has a remarks thread, and it is the one part of this page you
write rather than read. It is for the judgement that does not fit in a trait
score: *had a disease specific to my region, but I think this would look good
down in Texas where that pressure does not exist*.

You can **mention colleagues** in a remark. Anyone you tag gets an email with a
link straight to this variety's page, so the observation reaches the person who
can act on it — the sales rep or product development lead in the region you
think it belongs in — instead of sitting in the catalog waiting to be found.

Mentions only reach active users in your own organization, and tagging yourself
does not email you.

## Exporting a variety report

**Export PDF** builds a report from what is on screen, and you choose what goes
in: score chart, days to maturity chart, traits (all or a selected few),
evaluation photos, evaluation remarks, trial history, and trial locations.

<Callout variant="warning">
  The report covers at most **20 evaluations**. If your filters select more than
  that, narrow them — by region, or by date range — rather than expecting a
  longer document.
</Callout>
