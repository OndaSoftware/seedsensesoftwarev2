The varieties on a trial are what you are comparing. They appear in the trial
layout, and every evaluation scores against this list.

## Adding varieties

Open the trial, go to **Varieties**, and add from the variety catalog. If a
variety is not in the catalog yet, see [Add a variety](/varieties/add-a-variety)
or [Bulk add varieties](/varieties/bulk-add-varieties).

## The check variety

Mark the known commercial standard as your check. It is what everything else is
judged against, and it is called out separately in evaluation results and in the
variety detail charts.

## Changing the list later

You can add a variety mid-season. Removing one that already has evaluation
scores is a different matter — the scores go with it, so most teams leave it in
place and grade it as not recommended instead.
