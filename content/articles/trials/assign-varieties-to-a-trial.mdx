The varieties on a trial are what you are comparing. They appear in the trial
layout, and every evaluation scores against this list.

Open the trial, scroll to **Trial Varieties**, and choose **Manage Trial
Varieties**. Everything below happens there, and it is the same screen you come
back to whenever the list changes.

The varieties themselves come from the shared catalog in
[Data Settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings). This screen picks from that
catalog — it does not replace it.

## Two ways to add

**Multiple records (Table)** gives you a row per variety and is what you want
when you are entering a planting. **Single record** adds one at a time, which is
right for the one you forgot.

Either way each entry takes the same three things.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Planting number">
    The stake number — where this entry sits in the field. Start the check at
    zero and number the rest from one, and the evaluation screens will walk the
    trial in the order you planted it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The variety">
    Type to search. Breeder numbers work as well as names, so `BP777` finds it
    if that is how the seed is labelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Field Check and Variety Check">
    Two independent checkboxes — see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then **Create All**, and they are tied to the trial.

## Field Check and Variety Check

These are two separate flags, and the distinction matters more than it looks.

**Field Check** is the grower's own variety — what is already planted on that
ground. **Variety Check** is the standard you are measuring the trial against.

Very often they are the same plant, and in that case tick both. Sometimes they
are not: the grower runs one thing commercially and your programme benchmarks
against another. Ticking them separately is what lets the reports say which is
which.

Both flags matter downstream. Field and check varieties are the only entries a
supplier is allowed to see besides their own — see
[Export and email trial reports](/trials/reports-and-emails).

## When the variety is not in the system

If a breeder number has arrived that nobody has entered yet, you do not have to
leave the screen. **Add Data Variety** creates it in Data Settings from here,
and it becomes available to select immediately.

It asks for the same four things as
[Add a variety](/varieties/add-a-variety): name, classification, supplier, and
variety type. Names have to be unique across the organization — duplicates are
rejected rather than merged.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Search the catalog before you create anything. A variety entered twice under
  two spellings is painful to untangle once evaluations are attached to both,
  and the search box is right there.
</Callout>

## On the web, open the window fully

The web app expects a full screen. Squeeze it into half a monitor and columns
drop off this table; make it smaller still and the app tells you outright that
it does not work at that size. Nothing is broken — maximise the window.

## Changing the list later

You can add a variety mid-season. Removing one that already has evaluation
scores is a different matter — the scores go with it, so most teams leave it in
place and grade it *Not Recommended* or *Drop from Trialing* instead.
