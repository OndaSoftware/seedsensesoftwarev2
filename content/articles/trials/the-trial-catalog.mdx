**Trial Catalog** in the sidebar browses every trial in your organization. It is
the page you will spend the most time on, and four controls narrow it — they
stack.

## Status tabs

**Active**, **Completed**, and **All**. Active means trials in the ground that
have not been evaluated, completed, and wrapped up. A trial moves to Completed
when someone marks it complete on the trial itself — see
[Trial details and status](/trials/trial-details-and-status).

## Mine or everyone's

A separate toggle switches between all of the organization's trials and only the
ones you are the evaluator for. This is an ownership axis, not a status one, so
it combines with the tabs: Active + mine is the usual working view.

## Filters

Crop, evaluator, farmer, zone, ranch, field, trial type, and planting date. On
the web these are chips along the top; on mobile they are behind the filter
button.

**Zone** is the smallest geographical unit you have defined — "Southern
Bakersfield", "west of the 101 in Santa Maria", whatever ties together ground
that shares weather and soil. That is what makes it the useful filter when you
want trials that are genuinely comparable.

Filters combine, and they apply to the export too.

## Search

Free text over the trials, for when you already know the name. This is what the
ranch and field names on the trial are for — see
[Input a new trial](/trials/create-a-trial).

## On mobile, hit Sync

**The catalog does not refresh itself, even when you are online.** Sync is a
button you press. If a colleague added a trial an hour ago and you cannot see
it, sync before assuming anything is wrong.

Synced and unsynced views sit alongside the tabs for confirming nothing is still
sitting on a device — see
[Synced and unsynced trials](/data-and-sync/synced-and-unsynced-trials).

## Opening a trial

**Show Trial** opens the detail screen: everything that makes up the trial, plus
the routes into its tasks, varieties, layout, and evaluations.

**Open Map** hands the trial's coordinates to Google Maps and drops you straight
on the pin — the same pin whoever created the trial stood on. It is the fastest
way to turn a row in a list into directions.

## Exporting

**Export Catalog** produces a CSV of everything your filters have selected,
including the evaluation data for any trial that has been evaluated. Before it
runs, a summary shows every filter you have applied and how many trials that
comes to — read it, because what it lists is exactly what will be in the file.

Two limits worth knowing:

- The trial catalog exports **CSV only**. PDF export lives on the
  [evaluation catalog](/evaluations/the-evaluation-catalog), which is the
  right shape for a document you hand to someone.
- Export is **web only**. You can filter and browse the catalog on mobile, but
  the export button is not there.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Exporting the whole catalog is rarely what anyone wants. Filter to the crop
  and season first, confirm the count on the summary, then export.
</Callout>
