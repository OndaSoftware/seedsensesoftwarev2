**Trial Catalog** in the sidebar browses every trial in your organization. Four
controls narrow it, and they stack.

## Status tabs

**Active**, **Completed**, and **All**. A trial moves to Completed when someone
marks it complete on the trial itself — see
[Trial details and status](/trials/trial-details-and-status).

## Mine or everyone's

A separate toggle switches between all of the organization's trials and only the
ones you are the evaluator for. This is an ownership axis, not a status one, so
it combines with the tabs: Active + mine is the usual working view.

## Filters

Crop, evaluator, farmer, zone, ranch, field, trial type, and planting date. On
the web these are chips along the top; on mobile they are behind the filter
button.

Filters combine, and they apply to the export too — which is the point of
setting them up carefully before exporting.

## Search

Free text over the trials, for when you already know the name.

## Sync filters

Synced and unsynced views sit alongside the tabs, for confirming nothing is
still sitting on a device. See
[Synced and unsynced trials](/data-and-sync/synced-and-unsynced-trials).

## Exporting

**Export Catalog** offers CSV and PDF. Before it runs, a summary shows every
filter you have applied and how many trials that comes to — read it, because
what it lists is exactly what will be in the file.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Exporting the whole catalog is rarely what anyone wants. Filter to the crop
  and season first, confirm the count on the summary, then export.
</Callout>
