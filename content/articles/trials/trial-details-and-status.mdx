The trial detail screen is the hub for one trial. From here you reach its
varieties, its tasks, its layout, and its evaluations.

## What you can change later

Location, planting date, and the variety list can all be edited after a trial is
created. Changing the crop is the one edit to avoid, because traits and existing
evaluation scores are tied to it.

## Completing a trial

Mark a trial completed once the final evaluation is in. Completed trials drop
out of the active list and stop generating task reminders, but nothing is
deleted — you can still open, read, and export them.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Let the trial finish syncing before you mark it completed. Completing a trial
  that still has unsynced evaluations is the most common way teams lose track of
  a missing score.
</Callout>
