Three fields on a trial exist purely so somebody can find it again: latitude,
longitude, and driving instructions.

## The Trials Map

On mobile, **Trials Map** is the middle tab at the bottom of the screen. It
plots every trial you are the evaluator for, which makes it the fastest way to
see what is close to where you already are.

A trial only appears on the map if it has coordinates. If one is missing, open
the trial and fill in latitude and longitude — you can do that from the field,
standing on the plot.

## Driving instructions

Coordinates get you to a pin; driving instructions get you through the right
gate. This is a free text field on the trial, and it is worth writing for
anyone else who might have to cover the trial for you.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Write instructions for the person who has never been there. "Second turning
  after the packing shed, gate is usually chained" beats a repeat of the
  address.
</Callout>

## When the trial is not on your map

The map shows trials assigned to you as evaluator. If a colleague can see a
trial and you cannot, the evaluator on that trial is the thing to check — see
[A trial or variety is missing](/troubleshooting/missing-trials-or-varieties).
