A trial covers one planting at one location. **Trial Input** in the sidebar is
where you create one, and the same form is what you come back to when a detail
changes.

Everything the form asks for has to already exist in
[Data Settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings). If a dropdown is empty, that is
what it is telling you.

## Evaluator and planting date

The **evaluator** is not a formality — a trial's tasks appear on that person's
task list and nobody else's. Getting it wrong is the usual reason someone says
their home screen is empty.

The **planting date** is what the rest of the season is measured from.

## Crop and farmer

**Crop** decides which traits show up when the varieties are evaluated, so it is
not a casual change later. **Farmer** is the grower whose ground you are on, and
it also determines who receives the trial's reports by email.

## Location

**Zone** comes from the geographical hierarchy in Data Settings, and it is the
one location field you pick rather than type. **Ranch** and **Field** are free
text.

**Latitude** and **longitude** put the trial on the mobile Trials Map, and
**driving instructions** are for whoever has to find it at 6am.

## Trial type

Six types: Screening, Plot, Slotting, Strip, Harvest Pass, and Nursery Drop.

## Harvest date, chemical type, and notes

Estimated harvest date and chemical type are optional, but both are printed on
the trial map report, so filling them in saves explaining later. Notes are free
text for anything the fields do not cover.
