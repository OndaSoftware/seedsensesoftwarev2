Every trial has a **Reports & Emails** section at the bottom of its detail
screen. It does two things: it builds PDFs you can download, and it sends those
same PDFs out by email.

## The two reports

**Trial Map** is the plot layout — the map image with the trial's farmer, crop,
zone, planting date, harvest date, and chemical type printed alongside it. It is
the sheet you hand to someone walking the field.

**Evaluations Report** is the scoring: every evaluated variety with its traits,
score, pros, cons, conclusion, and photos. Check varieties are marked `(CH)` and
field varieties `(FV)` so a reader can see what everything was measured against.

## Downloading a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trial and scroll to Reports & Emails">
    On mobile, get there by tapping a task; on the web, from the trial catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Generate Trial Map or Generate Evaluations Report">
    The PDF is built on the server, so this needs a connection — with no signal
    the app says so instead of producing an empty file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the download">
    You will see "Downloading PDF", then a confirmation. On the web the file
    lands in your browser's downloads; on mobile it goes to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Emailing a report

**Send Emails** sends the same two reports without downloading them first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the email type">
    Trial Map or Evaluations Report — one per send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the recipients">
    Evaluator, Farmer, Supplier Reps, and Teams. Supplier Reps and Teams expand
    so you can tick individual suppliers or individual teams rather than all of
    them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on CC Evaluator">
    Leave this on to copy yourself on whatever goes out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    You need both an email type and at least one recipient before the Send
    button will do anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where each recipient's address comes from

This is worth knowing, because a missing address is the usual reason an email
does not arrive.

| Recipient | Address used |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluator | Your own account email |
| Farmer | Every address under Data Settings → Farmer Emails for the trial's farmer |
| Supplier Reps | Reps of the suppliers whose varieties are in this trial |
| Teams | Active members of the teams you ticked |

Supplier reps are filtered further: a rep is included only if they are marked
for all areas, or assigned to this trial's area under **Supplier Rep Areas**. A
rep who covers a different area will not be emailed about this trial, which is
usually what you want and occasionally a surprise.

<Callout variant="warning">
  "Error when sending email. Please verify that valid email addresses are
  defined on Data Settings" means the recipients you chose resolved to nothing.
  Check [Farmers, suppliers, and reps](/data-and-sync/farmers-suppliers-and-reps)
  before assuming the send failed.
</Callout>

## Exporting a whole catalog instead

The reports above cover one trial. To export many at once, use **Export
Catalog** in the [trial catalog](/trials/the-trial-catalog) or the
[evaluation catalog](/evaluations/the-evaluation-catalog) — both offer CSV and
PDF, and both export exactly what your current filters have selected.
