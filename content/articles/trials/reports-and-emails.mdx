Every trial can produce two PDFs, and send them out by email without you
downloading anything first.

On **mobile** they sit at the bottom of the trial detail screen. On the **web**
they are under the trial's **Actions** menu. Same two reports either way.

## The two reports

**Trial Map** is the plot layout — the map image with the trial's farmer, crop,
zone, planting date, harvest date, and chemical type printed alongside it. It is
the sheet you hand to someone walking the field, and most of the industry already
knows what one looks like.

**Evaluations Report** is the scoring: every evaluated variety with its traits
and trait attributes, score, pros, cons, conclusion, and photos. Check varieties
are marked `(CH)` and field varieties `(FV)` so a reader can see what everything
was measured against.

## Downloading a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trial">
    On mobile, scroll to the bottom; on the web, open **Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Generate Trial Map or Generate Evaluations Report">
    The PDF is built on the server, so this needs a connection — with no signal
    the app says so instead of producing an empty file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a moment">
    Both take a few seconds to build. When it finishes you can open it, and from
    there view, download, or print.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Marking the trial complete first is good housekeeping rather than a requirement —
you can generate either report at any point.

## Emailing a report

**Send Emails** sends the same two reports as a link. Recipients open the link to
view, download, or print; nothing arrives as an attachment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the email type">
    Trial Map or Evaluations Report — one per send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the recipients">
    Evaluator, Farmer, Supplier Reps, and Teams. Supplier Reps and Teams expand
    so you can tick individual suppliers or individual teams rather than all of
    them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on CC Evaluator">
    Leave this on. Large organizations run mail filters that quietly eat these
    messages, and a copy in your own inbox is how you find out the send worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    You need both an email type and at least one recipient before the Send
    button will do anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suppliers see only their own material

This is the most important thing on this page.

**A supplier rep receives a version of the report containing their own
varieties, plus the field variety and the check variety — and nothing else.**
Their competitors' entries are removed before the PDF is built.

So a trial with ten Sakata entries, two Enza entries, and one check produces a
Sakata report showing eleven rows and an Enza report showing three. Each supplier
sees what they need to judge their own material against the standard, and cannot
see what anyone else put in the ground.

<Callout variant="note">
  The filtering happens per recipient, so a single send to "all suppliers"
  produces a correctly trimmed report for each of them. You do not have to run
  the send once per supplier.
</Callout>

## Teams see everything

Teams are the opposite case. Whoever is on a team — internal or external — gets
the **full internal copy**, unedited, with nothing held back.

That is what teams are for: a sales team, a breeding team, West Coast product
development, everyone in PD. Set them up under
[Data Settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings) and they become a recipient group
you can tick.

Because a team can contain external addresses and still receives the unredacted
report, check who is on a team before using it for a send.

## Where each recipient's address comes from

A missing address is the usual reason an email does not arrive.

| Recipient | Address used |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluator | Your own account email |
| Farmer | Every address under Data Settings → Farmer Emails for the trial's farmer |
| Supplier Reps | Reps of the suppliers whose varieties are in this trial |
| Teams | Active members of the teams you ticked |

Supplier reps are filtered further: a rep is included only if they are marked
for all areas, or assigned to this trial's area under **Supplier Rep Areas**. A
rep who covers a different area will not be emailed about this trial, which is
usually what you want and occasionally a surprise.

<Callout variant="warning">
  "Error when sending email. Please verify that valid email addresses are
  defined on Data Settings" means the recipients you chose resolved to nothing.
  Check [Farmers, suppliers, and reps](/data-and-sync/farmers-suppliers-and-reps)
  before assuming the send failed.
</Callout>

## Exporting many trials instead

The reports above cover one trial. To export across trials, use **Export
Catalog** in the [trial catalog](/trials/the-trial-catalog) — CSV — or the
[evaluation catalog](/evaluations/the-evaluation-catalog) — CSV or PDF. Both
export exactly what your current filters have selected, and both are on the web
only.
