Photos sync after the data they belong to. A trial can show its scores on the
server while its photos are still on their way up.

## Give it a real connection

A full day of plot photos is a substantial upload. Leave the device on Wi-Fi
with the app open until the trial clears the unsynced filter.

## If they never arrive

If a trial sits unsynced overnight on a good connection, contact support before
doing anything drastic to the device — the photos are still on it, and that is
what makes recovery possible.
