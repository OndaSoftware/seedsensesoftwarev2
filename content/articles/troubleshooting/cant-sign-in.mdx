Work down this list in order — the first two account for most cases.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the email address">
    Sign-in only works with the exact address your invitation was sent to, not
    an alias or a personal address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset the password">
    Use Forgot password. The reset link goes to that same address, so check
    spam if it does not arrive within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you have a connection">
    Signing in requires a connection. Working offline is fine once you are
    signed in, but the first sign-in on a device is not.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the invitation was accepted">
    If you never set a password, your account exists but cannot sign in yet.
    Ask your administrator to resend the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Still stuck? [Contact support](/contact) with the email address you are trying
and the exact error text.
