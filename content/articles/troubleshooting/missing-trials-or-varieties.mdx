Something usually appears missing for one of these reasons rather than being
gone.

## It is filtered out

The most common cause. Check whether the catalog is filtered to active only, to
one crop, or to one sync state.

## It has not synced to your device

If a colleague created it recently and you have been offline, you do not have it
yet. Reconnect and let sync finish.

## It is completed, not deleted

Completed trials drop out of the active list. Switch the filter to completed.

## You do not have access to it

Access follows areas and teams. If it is none of the above, ask your
administrator whether the trial is in an area assigned to you.
