Unexpected results are usually explainable. Check these before treating it as a
bug.

## Which evaluator recorded it

Scores are stored per evaluator. A trial scored by two people has two sets, and
you may be looking at the other one.

## Which plot it came from

If the trial layout does not match how the trial was actually planted, scores
attach to the wrong variety. This is the single most common cause of a result
that makes no sense.

## What the inputs were

Check the trial's input records. A whole block scoring low is more often a
treatment or water story than a variety story.

## The grade is a judgement, not a calculation

Grades are set by a person, so a grade that does not match the trait scores is
not necessarily an error — see [Grades explained](/evaluations/grades-explained).

If none of that explains it, [contact support](/contact) with the trial name,
the variety, and the evaluator.
