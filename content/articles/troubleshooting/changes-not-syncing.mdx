A trial that will not clear the unsynced filter usually has one of three causes.

## 1. No usable connection

A weak connection is worse than none — the app may keep retrying against a
signal too poor to finish an upload. Move somewhere with real coverage or switch
to Wi-Fi.

## 2. A large photo queue

Photos are the biggest thing that syncs. A trial with many photos can take a
long time. Leave the app open and on a good connection rather than assuming it
has failed.

## 3. An actual error

If it stays stuck after a solid connection and a restart of the app, treat it as
an error and contact support.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Do not sign out, clear app data, or reinstall while work is unsynced. Those
  discard the local queue, and the work is not recoverable. Contact support with
  the device still as it is.
</Callout>
