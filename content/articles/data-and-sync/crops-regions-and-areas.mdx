Under **Settings → Data settings** sits the reference data every trial draws
on. It is worth getting right once.

## Crops and classifications

Crops are the top of the tree — traits and evaluations attach to them.
Classifications group crops for reporting.

## Coasts, regions, states, and areas

These nest, from broad to specific, and are how trials are located and how work
is divided between evaluators.

<Callout variant="tip">
  Set these up before creating trials. Adding a region later is easy; moving
  fifty existing trials into it is not.
</Callout>
