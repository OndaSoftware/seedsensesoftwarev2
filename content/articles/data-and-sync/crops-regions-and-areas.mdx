Under **Data Settings** sits the reference data every trial draws on. It is worth
getting right once.

## Crops and classifications

Three levels, narrowing as you go:

**Crop → Crop Classification → Variety**

The crop is *Pepper* or *Onion*. The classification is the commercial split
inside it — *Bell Pepper Green*, *Bell Pepper Yellow*, *Bell Pepper Red*,
*Jalapeño*, or for onions *Short Day* and *Long Day*. The variety is the
individual line you plant.

This is also how a variety knows its crop. You never set a crop on a variety
directly — it inherits one through its classification, which is why the
classification has to exist before the variety can.

Traits attach at the **crop** level, so every classification under *Pepper* is
evaluated on the same trait list. See [Crop traits](/evaluations/crop-traits).

## Geography: five nested levels

**Coast → Region → State → Area → Zone**

A trial is placed on a **zone**, which is the smallest and most specific unit —
"Southern Bakersfield" being everything south of a given highway, "western Santa
Maria" being west of the 101. Draw them so that everything inside one zone
shares its weather and soil, because that is what makes two trials in the same
zone genuinely comparable.

Ranch and field are then free text on the trial itself, not part of this
hierarchy.

Zones also drive **Supplier Rep Areas**, which decide who gets emailed about a
trial — see
[Export and email trial reports](/trials/reports-and-emails).

<Callout variant="tip">
  Set these up before creating trials. Adding a region later is easy; moving
  fifty existing trials into it is not.
</Callout>
