Everything you record is written to your device first and sent to the server
second. That order is the whole design: losing signal can never lose work.

## The cycle

When the app has a connection, it sends anything queued and pulls down changes
made by other people. This happens in the background — you do not have to
trigger it.

## What "synced" means

A trial marked synced has no local changes waiting. Unsynced means at least one
change is still queued on this device.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Unsynced work lives on one device. If that tablet is lost or wiped before it
  reconnects, the work goes with it. Sync before the end of a trip, not at the
  end of the week.
</Callout>

## When two people edit the same thing

Evaluations are stored per evaluator, so two people scoring the same trial do
not collide. For shared records like trial details, the most recent change wins.
