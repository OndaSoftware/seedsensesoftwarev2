Two sets of external people matter to a trial: the farmer providing the ground,
and the supplier providing the seed.

## Farmers

A farmer is attached to a trial's location. Keeping farmer email addresses
current matters because trial reports go to them.

## Suppliers and their reps

Varieties belong to suppliers, and suppliers have reps assigned to areas. That
association is what lets results be grouped and sent to the right person without
assembling a list by hand each season.
