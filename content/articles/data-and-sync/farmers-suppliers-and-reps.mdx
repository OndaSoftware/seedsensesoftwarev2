Two sets of external people matter to a trial: the farmer providing the ground,
and the supplier providing the seed. Both are set up in
[Data Settings](/data-and-sync/data-settings), and both feed the same thing —
who receives a report when you send one.

## Farmers

A farmer is attached to a trial, and **Farmer Emails** are the addresses that
receive its reports. A farmer can have several; a farmer with none is silently
skipped when you email a report, which is the usual reason a grower says they
never got it.

## Suppliers and their reps

Varieties belong to suppliers. **Supplier Reps** are the people at those
suppliers, and **Supplier Rep Areas** control which areas each rep covers.

A rep receives a trial's report only if they are marked for all areas, or
assigned to the area that trial sits in. That is what stops a Salinas rep being
emailed about a trial in Yuma, and it is worth checking when someone expected an
email and did not get one.

<Callout variant="note">
  Getting this association right is what lets a season's results reach the right
  person without anyone assembling a distribution list by hand.
</Callout>

## What a supplier actually receives

Worth knowing before you send anything: a supplier rep's copy of a report
contains **their own varieties plus the field and check varieties, and nothing
else**. Competitors' entries are stripped out before the PDF is built.

That is what makes it safe to send a mixed trial to every supplier in it at
once. See
[Export and email trial reports](/trials/reports-and-emails).
