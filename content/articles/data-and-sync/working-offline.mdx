SeedSense is built to be used where there is no connection. Recording work
offline is normal operation, not a fallback.

## What works offline

Opening trials, walking the layout, scoring evaluations, taking photos,
completing tasks, and creating new tasks all work with no signal.

## What needs a connection

Signing in for the first time on a device, pulling down trials created by
someone else, and uploading your work.

## Before you leave

Open the trials you will visit while you still have signal. That pulls their
current data down, so nothing is missing when you arrive.
