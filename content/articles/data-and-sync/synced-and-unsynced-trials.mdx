The trial catalog can filter by sync state. It is a thirty-second habit that
prevents the worst kind of data loss.

## The end-of-trip check

Before leaving a site — or at least before the end of the day — open the
catalog, filter to unsynced, and confirm the list is empty. If it is not, get to
a connection before the device goes in a drawer.

## If something stays unsynced

A trial that will not clear usually has a large photo queue or hit an error. See
[Changes are not syncing](/troubleshooting/changes-not-syncing).
