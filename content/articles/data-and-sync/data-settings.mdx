**Data Settings** holds everything a trial is assembled from. Nothing here is
field work — it is the lists that the trial form, the evaluation form, and the
email recipients all draw on. Get it right once and the rest of the season is
picking from dropdowns.

There is a search box at the top of the page; with eighteen options it is
usually faster than scanning.

## The six groups

### Crop Management

**Crops** are the top level. **Crop Classifications** sit under a crop, and
**Varieties** sit under a classification — which is how a variety knows which
crop it belongs to without you setting it directly. See
[Import varieties in bulk](/varieties/bulk-add-varieties).

### Farmer Management

**Farmers** are the growers whose ground you trial on. **Farmer Emails** are the
addresses that receive trial reports — a farmer can have several, and a farmer
with none simply gets skipped when you email a report.

### Geographical Data

Five levels, each one nested in the one before it:

**Coasts → Regions → States → Areas → Zones**

A trial is placed on a **zone**, then given a ranch and field name as free text.
So a zone has to exist before the trial can, and creating one means the area,
state, region, and coast above it already exist.

### Supplier Management

**Suppliers** provide the varieties you test. **Supplier Reps** are the people
at those suppliers, and **Supplier Rep Areas** control which areas each rep
covers. That last one decides who actually receives a trial report — see
[Export and email trial reports](/trials/reports-and-emails).

### Trait Management

**Traits** are what evaluators score, and **Trait Options** are the answers each
trait accepts. Traits are then attached to crops, so the evaluation form only
ever shows what is relevant to what is planted.

### Team Management

**Teams** group people, and **Team Members** are the people in them. Teams are a
recipient option when emailing reports, so they are worth setting up even in a
small organization.

## The order to build in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Geography, top down">
    Coasts, then regions, then states, then areas, then zones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Crops, then classifications">
    Varieties cannot be added until a classification exists for their crop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suppliers, then reps, then rep areas">
    Rep areas depend on both the reps and the areas existing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farmers, then farmer emails">
    An email row has to attach to a farmer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Traits, then trait options, then crop traits">
    Attaching traits to a crop is the step people forget, and it is the reason
    an evaluation form comes up empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Varieties last">
    They need a classification and a supplier to already be there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="tip">
  Data Settings is also on the mobile app's bottom bar, so a correction can be
  made in the field rather than waiting until you are back at a desk.
</Callout>
