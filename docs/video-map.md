# Walkthrough videos → articles

The nine recorded walkthroughs and the article each one belongs on. When a video
is published, replace `videoPending: true` in that article's frontmatter with
`youtubeId: "<11-char id>"` and add `videoDuration: "M:SS"`.

Video 8 covers two catalogs, so the same id goes on both of its articles.

| 7 | Managing Data Settings | `data-and-sync/data-settings.mdx` |

| 9 | Navigating the Variety Catalog | `varieties/the-variety-catalog.mdx` |

Set every one of these to **Unlisted** in YouTube Studio — this site is not
indexed, but a public video is public regardless.
